### Calculation 1.4: Anharmonic Potential Derivatives, Equilibrium, and Primary Stiffness ($k_0$)

* **A. What are we doing?**

* **C. How are we doing it?**  
  We compute the first derivative $\frac{dV}{dr}$ (the inter-particle restoring force $F(r) = -dV/dr$), solve for the zero-force equilibrium point $r_{\text{eq}}$, and compute the second derivative $\frac{d^2V}{dr^2}$ at $r_{\text{eq}}$ to determine the bond stiffness.

     $$\frac{dV}{dr} = V_0 \left[ \frac{d}{dr}\left( a_0^{12} r^{-12} \right) - 2 \frac{d}{dr}\left( a_0^6 r^{-6} \right) \right]$$

     Factoring out $a_0$:
     $$F(r) = -\frac{dV}{dr} = 12 \frac{V_0}{a_0} \left[ \left(\frac{a_0}{r}\right)^7 - \left(\frac{a_0}{r}\right)^{13} \right]$$

     $$\left(\frac{a_0}{r_{\text{eq}}}\right)^7 = \left(\frac{a_0}{r_{\text{eq}}}\right)^{13} \implies \left(\frac{a_0}{r_{\text{eq}}}\right)^6 = 1 \implies r_{\text{eq}} = a_0$$

     $$V(a_0) = V_0 [ 1^{12} - 2(1^6) ] = V_0 [ 1 - 2 ] = -V_0$$
  3. **Second Derivative (Bond Stiffness $k_0$):**

  5. **Explicit Numerical Calculation of $k_0$:**

     - $72 V_0 = 72 \times (2.716780 \times 10^8\text{ J}) = 1.9560816 \times 10^{10}\text{ J}$

       $$k_0 = \frac{1.9560816 \times 10^{10}\text{ J}}{2.612280 \times 10^{-70}\text{ m}^2} = \mathbf{7.488024 \times 10^{79}\text{ N/m}}$$

### Calculation 1.6: Cauchy-Born Lattice Elasticity & Exact Emergence of the Speed of Light ($c_t \equiv c$)

* **A. What are we doing?**

* **B. Based on what?**

* **C. How are we doing it?**  
  We compute the strain energy density $W(\varepsilon)$, sum the tensor products of the 12 nearest-neighbor vectors $\mathbf{R}_n$, perform Voigt orientation averaging to obtain the isotropic macroscopic shear modulus $\mu_0$, and substitute $\mu_0$ and $\rho_0$ into the acoustic shear wave equation $c_t = \sqrt{\mu_0 / \rho_0}$.
* **D. Step-by-Step Mathematical Execution:**
  1. The 12 nearest-neighbor vectors $\mathbf{R}_n$ of the HCP/FCC lattice with bond length $a_0$ are:
     $$\mathbf{R}_n \in \left\{ \frac{a_0}{\sqrt{2}}(\pm 1, \pm 1, 0), \; \frac{a_0}{\sqrt{2}}(\pm 1, 0, \pm 1), \; \frac{a_0}{\sqrt{2}}(0, \pm 1, \pm 1) \right\}$$

     $$\frac{\mu_0}{\rho_0} = \frac{\frac{72}{5\sqrt{2}} \frac{V_0}{a_0^3}}{\frac{\sqrt{2} M_0}{a_0^3}} = \frac{72}{5 \times (\sqrt{2} \cdot \sqrt{2})} \frac{V_0}{M_0} = \frac{72}{5 \times 2} \frac{V_0}{M_0} = \frac{72}{10} \frac{V_0}{M_0} = \mathbf{7.2 \frac{V_0}{M_0}}$$

### Calculation 1.7: The Longitudinal Mode Cutoff and the $10^{28}$ Phonon Energy Gap
