A. Reversible substrate + low-entropy start (exact bit arithmetic, HPP lattice gas)
==============================================================================
128x128 lattice, 4 velocity channels, 16245 particles, all in the left half at t = 0
micro-rule: head-on pairs scatter by 90 degrees, then everything streams -- a BIJECTION (reversible)
coarse-grained entropy (8x8 blocks): t = 0: 22272.6   t = 600: 35432.7   (maximum for this density ~ 35969) nats
reverse every velocity at t = 600 and run the SAME law 600 more steps:
   returns bit-for-bit to the initial state: True;  entropy back to 22272.6
   with ONE of the 65536 bits flipped at the turn-around: 11231 bits differ at the end; entropy ends at 31647.1
entropy rise Delta S = 13160 nats: of all microstates looking like the mixed state, a fraction
   exp(-Delta S) = 10^(-5715) would un-mix by themselves.
=> the substrate's LAW has no arrow (exact reversal works). The arrow comes from the START (low
   entropy) plus coarse-graining, and 'going back' needs control of every bit: one wrong bit fails.
   (HPP is only weakly chaotic, so the flipped run partly un-mixes; a fully chaotic law is worse.)

B. A growing substrate: one-way order, but no universal 'now' (causal-set growth)
==============================================================================
transitive percolation (Rideout & Sorkin, PRD 61, 024002 (2000)), 14 elements, p = 0.18:
   23 causal relations; acyclic (nothing precedes itself, no loops): True
   number of birth orders that produce exactly this causal set: 9539712 (out of 87178291200 orderings)
=> 'one way' is built into the substrate: the relation 'is to the past of' has no loops, so no
   chain of influences can return to its start. Yet the order of birth is not physical --
   9539712 different sequences give the same universe (discrete general covariance). There is a
   direction of becoming, but no global moment 'now': simultaneity is relative, as in relativity.
   checks: a 6-chain has 1 birth order (fully ordered); a 6-antichain has 720 = 6!

=> nature has ONE microscopic arrow (the weak interaction's T violation, tiny but 14 sigma; CPT

   been seen; quantum experiments can make the ORDER of two events indefinite, but not circular.

D. What this means for the substrate
==============================================================================
* If the substrate's law is reversible (a unitary quantum network, USRT v2), the arrow must come
  from its initial state (as in A): the substrate started extremely ordered. That is an INPUT.
* If the substrate GROWS (causal-set substrate, r19), 'one way' is fundamental: the past is
  fixed, the future is being added, no loops are possible -- exactly the user's 'cannot go back'.
  It still does not explain the low-entropy start; both ingredients are needed.
* The tiny weak-interaction arrow must be a property of how matter-knots sit in the substrate
  (it distinguishes matter from antimatter); its origin is open in every theory.
