H2a. Falling, measured: sound and a soliton in a 1D medium held in a harmonic trap
==============================================================================

at omega (acceleration ratio 1).  Plain GP: i psi_t = [-psi''/2 + V + |psi|^2] psi (hbar = m = 1, c_s^2 = n, Gamma = 1).
    ground state (imaginary-time relaxation): chemical potential 8.018, central density 8.043, Thomas-Fermi radius 40.0;
      residual of the GP equation 1.1e-02 (relative to mu, |x| < 30).
    dark soliton released at rest at x = 15: fit x = A cos(Omega t + phi): A = 15.32, Omega/omega = 0.7084  (1/sqrt2 = 0.7071);
      acceleration relative to a decoupled mass = (Omega/omega)^2 = 0.502   [Busch & Anglin 2000: 1/2 exactly]
    sound packet, amplitude 0.4 %, width 1.5: speed 2.905 near x = 0 and 2.499 near x = 22 (sqrt(n): 2.836, 2.363) -> Gamma = 0.907
    sound packet, amplitude 0.4 %, width 3: speed 2.860 near x = 0 and 2.565 near x = 22 (sqrt(n): 2.836, 2.363) -> Gamma = 0.661

    logarithmic medium (mu = kappa ln n, c_s^2 = kappa = 8 everywhere, Gamma = 0): exact Gaussian ground state n = exp(-a x^2), a = 6.250e-04;
      the solver keeps it stationary to 3.3e-06 over t = 5.
    sound packet, amplitude 0.4 %, width 1.5: speed 2.860 near x = -2 and 2.874 near x = 18 (c_s = 2.828) -> Gamma = -0.056
    sound packet, amplitude 0.4 %, width 3: speed 2.806 near x = -2 and 2.740 near x = 18 (c_s = 2.828) -> Gamma = 0.255
      Gamma = 0.10 +- 0.16   [prediction 0]; for comparison a plain-GP medium gives 0.78 with the same method.
    => inertial-frame fall rates in units of the medium's own acceleration g (flow-gravity reading of the trap):
       decoupled mass 0 (it stays inertial);  GP sound 1 - Gamma/2 = 0.61;  GP dark soliton 1 - 0.502 = 0.50;  constant-c_s sound 0.95.

==============================================================================
H2b. Falling, 3D: the added-mass table (inviscid, incompressible, body much larger than the healing length)
==============================================================================
A sphere of volume V and density x*rho in a flow whose parcels accelerate at g feels the pressure-gradient force rho V g and the
added-mass reaction C_m rho V (g - a):  x rho V a = rho V g + C_m rho V (g - a)  =>  a / g = (1 + C_m) / (x + C_m).
    C_m of a sphere from the kinetic energy of its potential flow (numerical quadrature): 0.5000   (exact 1/2).
      density of the lump / medium  fall rate a / g
                    cavity (x = 0)            3.000
                           x = 0.5            1.500
            neutral parcel (x = 1)            1.000
                             x = 2            0.600
                            x = 10            0.143
                         x = 1e+06            0.000
    A cavity falls three times as fast as the medium, a heavy lump hardly falls, and only a lump with the density of the medium itself
    follows the flow.  Fall rate depends on what the object is made of: the equivalence principle fails at order one unless every kind of
    matter has x = 1 -- i.e. unless matter is not a lump in the medium but an excitation carried by it.
    Size of the effect if a lump's x tracked its composition only through nuclear binding: Ti vs Pt differ by 0.80 MeV per nucleon = 8.6e-04 of the mass,
    so delta a / a = |d ln a / dx| delta x = 0.67 x 8.6e-04 = 5.7e-04.  MICROSCOPE (Ti vs Pt): |eta| = (1.5e-15 +- 2.5e-15), typed from memory.
    The effect would exceed the measurement by a factor ~2e+11.  (Illustration only: it assumes x responds to binding energy at order one.)
