
==============================================================================
R1. Selection rule: a single spin quantum cannot decay into density phonons
==============================================================================
Hydrodynamic energy density of the two-component medium (N = N0 + a total density, z imbalance, phi relative phase, v_d total-phase gradient, w = grad phi), quantum pressure dropped:
    H = N v_d^2/2 + N w^2/8 + N z v_d w/2 + (g + g12) N^2/4 + (g - g12) N^2 z^2/4 + Omega N (1 - sqrt(1 - z^2) cos phi).
    Exchange 1 <-> 2 (z -> -z, phi -> -phi, w -> -w): H unchanged: True.
    Cubic part (7 terms): N0*a*g*z**2/2 - N0*a*g12*z**2/2 + N0*v_d*w*z/2 + Omega*a*phi**2/2 + Omega*a*z**2/2 + a*v_d**2/2 + a*w**2/8
    Terms with an odd number of spin fields (z, phi, w): 0.  => every cubic vertex has 0 or 2 spin fields:  d-d-d, d-spin-spin  (no spin-d-d).
    Therefore:  spin -> d + d is forbidden, spin -> d (one-phonon conversion) is forbidden, spin + spin -> d is allowed, and the lightest spin quantum is STABLE (a dark-matter-like species).
    The vertices of spin + spin -> d are not suppressed by Omega: a w^2 / 8 (coefficient 1/8), N0 v_d z w / 2 (coefficient N0/2), (g - g12) N0 a z^2 / 2 and Omega a (z^2 + phi^2) / 2.
