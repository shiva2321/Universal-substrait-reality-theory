V2. The compatibility problem: a slow spin sound needs a LONG-range inter-sheet repulsion
==============================================================================
    Sheets 26 healing lengths apart (the middle of the range above).  A contact interaction between the sheets is exponentially small there (g12 / g ~ e^(-d/xi) ~ 5e-12): s = 1,
    excluded by r44.  A mediator of range lambda_m gives g12 / g ~ exp(-(d - t) / lambda_m) (t = sheet thickness ~ 1 healing length; the exact plane-plane integral of a Yukawa potential differs by a power-law
    prefactor).  With s^2 = (g - g12)/(g + g12):

    (mediator mass = hbar c / lambda_m = M_P / (lambda_m / xi) for xi = l_P; 1 M_P = 1.22e+19 GeV.)
    => a spin sound of 0.2-0.5 c needs a mediator of range 49-313 healing lengths, i.e. mass 3.9e+16-2.5e+17 GeV for alpha = 1: 49-313 times longer than the
       wavefunction tail that sets the tunnelling.  A short-range tunnelling suppression and a long-range repulsion can come from the same bulk only if the repulsion is carried by a field much lighter
       than the one that governs the matter wavefunction: two different bulk ranges, as for a nearly massless (gravity-like) bulk force between the sheets.  That is a requirement, not a derivation.

V3. Technical naturalness
==============================================================================
    Omega: when Omega = 0 the relative phase has an exact shift symmetry (r39), so every quantum correction to Omega is proportional to Omega: a small Omega is stable under renormalisation
    (technically natural).  The exponential tunnelling origin gives it without tuning.
    s: s = 1 (g12 = 0, two decoupled components) and s = 0 (g12 = g, the edge of the miscible phase) are the special points; s = 0.3 means g12 / g = 0.83, close to the second one:
    a mild tuning that no symmetry protects (unless g12 and g are fixed by one mediator, V2).
