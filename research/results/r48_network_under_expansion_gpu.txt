==============================================================================
    Energy fraction in strings in the radiation era: Omega_s = rho_s / rho_c = (32 pi / 3) G mu / xi^2 = 15.8 G mu  (xi = 1.46); it is constant in time for a scaling network (here to within the drift above).

    so strings would hold the energy of the universe: no decay brings it down.  A scaling network is not diluted by waiting; only inflation after the strings form (or their absence) removes it.
    (Global strings have a logarithmically growing tension, mu = 2 pi v^2 ln(xi t / core), and radiate Goldstone quanta, so the energy fraction grows with ln(t / core) at fixed xi: worse than this estimate.)
