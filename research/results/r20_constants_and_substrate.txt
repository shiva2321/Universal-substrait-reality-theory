A. Units versus numbers
==============================================================================
c, hbar, G (and k_B, epsilon_0) only convert between units; change them together with your rulers
and clocks and nothing observable changes (Duff, Okun & Veneziano, JHEP 03 (2002) 023).
In the substrate picture they are the CELLS' properties:
   c    = maximum signal speed of the cells (r05) -- or the cone structure itself (r19)
   hbar = quantum of action per cell (r06, r17)
   G    = l_P^2 c^3/hbar, with the cell size a = 0.31 sqrt(N) l_P (r14): l_P = 1.6163e-35 m
What physics actually measures are pure numbers. The ones that build our world:
   alpha (electromagnetism)                   7.297353e-03   [1/137.035999177(21)]
   alpha_G = G m_p^2/(hbar c) (gravity)       5.906149e-39   [rel. unc. 2e-5 (from G)]
   beta = m_e/m_p                             5.446170e-04   [1/1836.152673426(32)]
   alpha_s(M_Z) (strong force)                1.180000e-01   [+- 0.0009]
   Lambda l_P^2 (cosmological constant)       2.800000e-122   [Planck 2018]
   eta = baryons/photons                      6.100000e-10   [Planck 2018 / BBN]
The Standard Model + gravity + cosmology needs about 31 such numbers (Tegmark, Aguirre, Rees &
Wilczek, PRD 73, 023505 (2006)). No accepted theory derives any of them from first principles.

B. How the numbers build the world (dimensional estimates, then real objects)
==============================================================================
atom size     a0 = hbar/(m_e c alpha)            = 5.2918e-11 m      (Bohr radius, exact definition)
atom energy   alpha^2 m_e c^2 / 2               = 13.6057 eV     (hydrogen ionisation 13.6 eV)
star mass     alpha_G^(-3/2) m_p                = 3.685e+30 kg = 1.85 solar masses
   Chandrasekhar limit 3.098 (hbar c/G)^(3/2) / (mu_e m_p)^2 = 1.435 M_sun (measured white dwarfs: <= ~1.4)
largest cold planet  (alpha/alpha_G)^(3/2) m_p   = 2.297e+27 kg = 1.21 Jupiter masses
number of protons in a star ~ alpha_G^(-3/2)    = 2.20e+57
=> two numbers, alpha ~ 1e-2 and alpha_G ~ 1e-39, set the sizes of atoms, planets and stars
   (Carr & Rees, Nature 278, 605 (1979); Press & Lightman, Phil. Trans. R. Soc. A 310, 323 (1983)).
   A star holds ~ alpha_G^(-3/2) ~ 1e57 protons BECAUSE gravity is 1e39 times weaker than the
   other forces. These are real, quantitative relations between constants and structure.

D. Why gravity is so weak: an exponential from a modest number (dimensional transmutation)
==============================================================================
run 1/alpha_s up to the Planck energy (one loop, 6 flavours): 1/alpha_s(M_P) ~ 52.4
the strong scale where alpha_s blows up: Lambda ~ M_P exp(-2 pi / (b alpha_s(M_P)))
   d ln(m_p) / d(1/alpha_s(M_P)) = -2 pi / b = -0.90: a 10% change in 1/alpha_s(M_P) changes m_p by x110 and alpha_G by x1e+04
   alpha_G = (m_p/m_P)^2 = 5.91e-39: the 1e-39 is the square of an exponential of ~52 x (2 pi/7)
=> the 'hierarchy' between gravity and the other forces is not a coincidence to be fitted: it is the
   logarithmic running of the strong coupling (Wilczek; Gross, Politzer 1973). The original USRT
   formula m_e ~ m_P exp(-3/(8 alpha)) imitated this, but with the wrong coupling and a fitted exponent.

E. Large-number coincidences, and whether the constants are constant
==============================================================================
Dirac's numbers: electric/gravitational force between p and e  N1 = 2.27e+39
                 age of universe / light-crossing time of an electron  N2 = 4.63e+40
                 protons in the Hubble volume  Nb = 2.7e+78  (vs N1^2 = 5.1e+78)
Dirac (1937) guessed N1 ~ N2 always, so G ~ 1/t: predicted dG/dt / G = -1/t0 = -7.2e-11 per year.
Lunar laser ranging (Williams, Turyshev & Boggs, PRL 93, 261101 (2004)): (4e-13 +- 9e-13) per year -> Dirac excluded at 81 sigma
Dicke (Nature 192, 440 (1961)): N1 ~ N2 because observers exist when stars shine, which ties t to
   alpha_G -- a selection effect, not a law.
alpha today: atomic clocks give d(alpha)/dt / alpha = (1.0e-18 +- 1.1e-18) per year (Lange et al.,
   PRL 126, 011102 (2021)): over the age of the universe at most ~4e-08.
=> whatever the substrate is, the ratios of its couplings are FROZEN to ~1e-8 over cosmic history.
