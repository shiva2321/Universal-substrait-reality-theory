==============================================================================
S4. What this means for the Planck-length medium
==============================================================================
    In the r33 mapping the chemical potential is mu = m c^2 = m_P c^2 / (sqrt2 alpha) and the healing length is alpha l_P.  A barrier of U_c ~ a few mu on the scale of one
    healing length is a potential of order 1.7 x m_P c^2/(sqrt2 alpha) = 1.2 m_P c^2 / alpha varying over alpha l_P: a Planck-scale external structure, not a weak field.
    A second component is an internal degree of freedom (it needs its own population N_b of order the wall's deficit) and is the better-motivated stabiliser; whether it is enough is in S3.
    Not computed: a conserved topological charge (e.g. a winding of a second field around the wall) and what sources the escape-speed flow of r34: still open.
