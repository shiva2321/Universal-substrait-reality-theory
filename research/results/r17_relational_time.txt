A. Relational time (Page-Wootters): a static universe whose parts see time pass
==============================================================================
clock: 16 evenly spaced levels; system: a qubit; constraint J = H_clock + H_system
physical states (J Psi = 0): 2-dimensional;  |J Psi| = 5.7e-17
the WHOLE universe does not change: max | |<Psi|exp(-iJs)|Psi>| - 1 | = 4.4e-16
but the system, read relative to the clock, evolves exactly by Schroedinger's equation:
   max infidelity over 16 clock readings = 7.8e-16
=> time is a RELATION between parts (clock reading <-> system state), not an external
   parameter. Realised with entangled photons: Moreva et al., PRA 89, 052122 (2014).

==============================================================================
   well depth A = 0.0 (local rate N0 = 1.000): tick rate seen from far away: covariant medium 1.00002, r08 medium 1.00002
   well depth A = 0.1 (local rate N0 = 0.900): tick rate seen from far away: covariant medium 0.90026, r08 medium 1.00016
   well depth A = 0.2 (local rate N0 = 0.800): tick rate seen from far away: covariant medium 0.80051, r08 medium 1.00025
   well depth A = 0.3 (local rate N0 = 0.700): tick rate seen from far away: covariant medium 0.70076, r08 medium 1.00029
=> covariant medium: clocks deep in the well tick slower by exactly N0 (residual ~3e-4 from the
   well's curvature over the clock's size) = the gravitational redshift. r08's medium (only
   the wave speed lowered): NO redshift. Measurement (Galileo 5 & 6, Delva et al. 2018):
   redshift violation alpha = (1.90e-06 +- 2.48e-05)
   covariant medium alpha = 0: 0.08 sigma; r08 medium alpha = -1: 4.0e+04 sigma -> EXCLUDED
   Correction to r08/E12: trapped energy must lower the wave speed AND every internal rate by the
   same factor N(x) (the lapse). Free fall stays universal (Killing energy 8 m N(x)), and now
   clocks redshift correctly. 'Time runs at the local rate of the substrate.'
