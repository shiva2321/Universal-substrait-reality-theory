D. Polarisations and universal coupling
==============================================================================

E. The obstacle and its loopholes
==============================================================================
Weinberg-Witten (1980): a theory with a Lorentz-covariant, conserved T^{mu nu} cannot contain a
  massless particle of helicity |h| > 1 that carries energy-momentum. So the graviton cannot
  be an ordinary bound state (e.g. two photons) living in a pre-existing flat spacetime.
Loopholes, each tried in the next scripts:
  1. gauge route (r12): the graviton is a gauge mode; its own T is not gauge-covariant (as in GR).
  2. induced route (r13): Sakharov 1967 - the substrate's quantum jiggling gives spacetime stiffness.
  3. entanglement route (r14): Jacobson 1995 - Einstein's equations from entanglement area law.
  4. defect route (r15): Kroener/Kleinert world crystal - curvature = density of disclinations.
  5. Lorentz symmetry only emergent (lattice), or an extra dimension (holography) - both evade the
     theorem's assumptions.
