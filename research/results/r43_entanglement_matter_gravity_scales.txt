M1. The ladder: from an electron to the Hubble sphere (arithmetic from constants)
==============================================================================
    Constants: l_P = 1.616e-35 m, m_P = 2.176e-08 kg, H0 = 67.4 km/s/Mpc  =>  c / H0 = 1.37e+26 m, mass inside c^3/(2 G H0) = 9.24e+52 kg.
    Compton = reduced Compton wavelength hbar/(M c);  compactness = 2 G M / (R c^2);  E_G/hbar = G M^2 / (R hbar) is both the self-gravity rate
    (Diosi-Penrose decoherence time = hbar/E_G) and the rate at which two copies of the object at distance ~R would gain gravitational phase;
    S_surf = pi R^2 / l_P^2 (nats) is the medium's entanglement across the surface (area law with Bekenstein-Hawking coefficient, E19); S_BH = pi r_s^2 / l_P^2.

    Reading:
      * Compton/R is 1 for the electron (its size is taken to be its reduced Compton wavelength), 0.25 for the proton and below 1e-9 for everything larger: for
        every larger object the quantum character lives in superposition and entanglement of states, not in the size of the object.
      * Compactness stays below 1e-8 until a neutron star (0.35) -- gravity is a weak-field theory everywhere we measure it; the Hubble sphere sits exactly at 1 by
        construction (M = c^3/(2 G H0)): the observable universe is at its own Schwarzschild threshold, a standard identity, not a coincidence of this programme.
      * The medium's entanglement across an object's surface exceeds the Bekenstein-Hawking entropy of the same mass by (R/r_s)^2 = 1/compactness^2: the area-law entropy is
        not 'used up' by ordinary matter; only black holes (and the Hubble sphere) saturate it.  Matter concentration enters only through how close R is to r_s.
      * E_G/hbar is 62 /s for the QGEM-scale grain (self-gravity time 16 ms, of the order of the experiment's duration), 1e10 /s for a dust speck
        and 1e28 /s for a person: the scale where gravity-mediated entanglement (E21: 0.03-2.8 rad) becomes visible.  If quantum states collapsed by self-gravity
        (Diosi-Penrose; the parameter-free version was excluded by Donadi et al. 2021, E21), heavier objects could not keep them for longer than hbar/E_G.
      * The quantum correction to Newton's law at the surface, (41/10 pi)(l_P/R)^2 (E21), is 2e-45 for the electron's Compton radius and falls to
        2e-122 at the Hubble radius.

M2. Matter concentration and entanglement: the first law of entanglement on a lattice
==============================================================================
    Lattice scalar, 200 sites, mass^2 = 0.01, region A = sites 96..103 (8 sites).  Vacuum entropy S_A = 0.74513 nats.
    Self-checks of the modular Hamiltonian: S = <K> + ln Z  (thermodynamic identity): 0.74513 vs 0.74513;  <K0>_vacuum = sum eps nu = 48.06951 vs trace formula 48.06951.
    (Region of 8 sites, correlation length 1/m = 10 sites.  A first attempt with a 40-site region at m^2 = 0.25 failed: the deep-interior entanglement-spectrum
    values nu - 1/2 fall below double precision (1e-16) there, so the modular Hamiltonian is numerical noise; all nu - 1/2 here exceed 1e-12.)
    Matter lump: mass^2 raised by lambda exp(-(i - i0)^2 / (2 sigma^2)), sigma = 2 sites (a localized matter concentration), lump at the centre of A.

    => at small lambda delta S and delta <K0> agree (ratio 1.0018 at lambda = 0.0001); they go as lambda^0.98 and lambda^0.94 (first order); the relative entropy delta<K0> - delta S ~ lambda^1.96
       (second order) and is never negative (positivity: the vacuum is the entropy-maximising state for given modular energy).
    Where the lump sits (lambda = 0.01): delta S of region A against the lump position i0:
      lump at the centre of A: delta S = -3.01e-02;  lump 20 sites away: -1.00e-03;  lump 40 or more sites away: |delta S| <= 1.7e-05.
      The sign and size of the effect: a heavier local field is less correlated, so matter inside or beside the region changes its entanglement; the response dies off over a
      few correlation lengths (1/m = 10 sites): it is local, which is what lets it behave like a field equation.
    Meaning for the fluid picture: a matter concentration is a local change of the medium's Hamiltonian; the entanglement of any region changes by delta<K> to first
    order.  For a region bounded by a Rindler/Unruh horizon K is the boost energy and delta S = delta<K> is the Clausius relation  delta S = delta Q / T  that Jacobson (1995)
    turns into Einstein's equation.  What is verified here is only the lattice first law in a free field: not Einstein's equation.

M3. Stretching entangles: the phonon pairs of the expanding medium (r37 mode integration)
==============================================================================
In de Sitter the phonon pair (k, -k) is a two-mode squeezed state.  With u'' + [1 + (lambda^2/2) tau^2 - 2/tau^2] u = 0 (tau = -c k eta, lambda = H xi/c, r37) and the
adiabatic vacuum at early times, the number of quanta in each mode at tau is  n = (|u'|^2 + |u|^2)/2 - 1/2  and the entanglement entropy of the pair is
S = (n+1) ln(n+1) - n ln n.  For lambda = 0 the exact solution gives n = 1 / (4 tau^4).

    => lambda = 0: n agrees with 1/(4 tau^4) to 8e-09; for tau < 0.3 the pair entropy grows by 4.00 nats per e-fold after horizon exit (a squeezing
       parameter r = 2 ln(aH/k), S = 2 r); with the medium's graininess (lambda = 0.3) the same slope, 4.00: the entanglement is not an artefact of the continuum.
    Entropy of all pairs per Hubble volume (de Sitter, exact n = 1/(4 tau^4); pairs counted once): S_H = I / (3 pi), I = int tau^2 S(n(tau)) d tau = 2.060  =>  S_H = 0.219 nats,
    independent of H: a constant, small amount of entanglement per Hubble volume, produced and diluted continuously.
    Compare with the horizon's own area-law entropy S_dS = 8 pi^2 (M_Pl/H)^2  (M_Pl = reduced Planck mass):
                                 epoch    H (GeV)       S_dS  S_H / S_dS
          inflation (r39 plateau, r37)    1.0e+13    4.7e+12     4.7e-14
    end of the quench (r35 scale, H ~ 1e17)    1.0e+17    4.7e+04     4.7e-06
                  dark-energy era (H0)    1.4e-42   2.3e+122    9.7e-124
    So the pairs made by stretching carry entanglement of order 0.1 nat per Hubble volume, while the horizon's area-law entanglement is 1e12 to 1e122 nats: the
    structure-seeding pairs are an exceedingly small part of the medium's entanglement.  The entanglement that matters for G and for horizon entropy is the
    short-distance one, E19, not the cosmological pairs.
