==============================================================================

   Delta V_relative(z) = (1 / sqrt(N_patches(z))) * (Delta V / V)_patch.
   => Causal filter kernel:  K_horizon(z) = 1 / sqrt(N_patches(z)) = 1 / [2 d_C(z) a(z) H(z)].
