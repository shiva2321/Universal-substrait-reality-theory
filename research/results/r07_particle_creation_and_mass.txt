B. Why trapped energy IS mass: light in a box
==============================================================================
A pulse of light (energy E) bouncing between two mirrors:
  * push the box: the forward-moving mirror reflects blue-shifted light, the back mirror red-shifted;
    the net momentum of the trapped light is P = gamma E v / c^2 -> inertial mass = E/c^2.
  * hang it in gravity g: light at the bottom has energy higher by a factor (1 + g L/c^2), so the
    bottom mirror is pushed harder: net weight = (E/c^2) g -> gravitational mass = E/c^2.
  1 joule of trapped light adds 1.11e-17 kg to the box, and weighs exactly that much.

C. Real-world evidence that mass is trapped energy
==============================================================================
deuteron: m_p + m_n - m_d = 2.224566 MeV  (CODATA 2022 masses)
  = the energy released (2.2246 MeV gamma) when a neutron is captured by a proton: binding the two
  traps less energy, so the bound system weighs less, exactly by E/c^2.
  Direct test: Rainville et al., Nature 438, 1096 (2005) confirmed E = mc^2 to 4 parts in 10^7.
proton: quark rest masses 2 m_u + m_d = 9.0 MeV vs proton 938.3 MeV -> 99% of the proton's mass is trapped field energy
  (lattice QCD reproduces hadron masses from this trapped energy: Durr et al., Science 322, 1224 (2008)).
So ordinary matter is overwhelmingly trapped energy: this part of the user's intuition is exactly
how nature works.

D. Where large mass ratios come from: dimensional transmutation
==============================================================================
one-loop QCD scale: Lambda = M_Z exp(-2 pi / (b0 alpha_s)), b0 = 23/3, alpha_s(M_Z) = 0.118 +- 0.0009
  -> Lambda = 88 MeV  (range 83-93 MeV); two-loop MS-bar value ~210 MeV
  an O(0.1) coupling gives a scale 1038x below M_Z through an EXPONENTIAL; the proton mass
  (~4-5 x the two-loop Lambda) is set this way. The original USRT electron formula m_e ~ m_P exp(-3/(8 alpha))
  imitated this mechanism, but here the exponent comes from a measured running coupling, not a fit.
