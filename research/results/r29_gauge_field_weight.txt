C. Where the 'two scalars' claim comes from, and whether it survives
==============================================================================
   r26 argues that in Coulomb gauge the two transverse polarizations obey the scalar dispersion, so the reduced density
   matrix 'factors' into two scalar copies. Direct test: entropy of the Coulomb-gauge link variables (A^T, E^T) in the cube.
      links inside the cube only:        eta = 0.04085 = 1.69 scalars
      links inside and crossing:         eta = 0.03030 = 1.25 scalars
   Neither is 2, and they differ from each other: the transverse projector is non-local, so restricting gauge-fixed link
   variables to a region has no unique meaning and the density matrix does not factor. The gauge-invariant electric-centre
   quantum entropy is 1.03 scalars. The statement 'w_V = 2.0 identically' is not supported.
   r26's own printed edge-mode value (1.2455 nats per face, from the spectral density of the normal flux) agrees with the
   1.2461 computed here from whole cubes; but r26 then leaves it out of its ledger although its text says S_total = 2 S_scalar + S_Shannon.

Verdict
==============================================================================
* COMPUTED HERE: the quantum (bulk) entanglement of the free Maxwell field across a cube face is 1.03 scalars, not 2
  (eta_sym = 0.02495 against 0.02421); the gauge-invariant boundary also carries a classical flux entropy of
  1.25 + ln(1/g) nats per face, about 51+ scalars per vector boson at flux quantum 1.
* VALIDATED: 2+1D agreement with an independent dual-scalar calculation to 1e-13; Gauss-law and Bianchi null spaces exact;
  box size changes the result by < 1e-4.
* THE QUESTION THAT DECIDES R6: does the classical flux-sector entropy count towards the gravitational area coefficient?
  If not: N_eff(SM) = 222 at r = 1 (inside 180-360). If yes: about 1155, 3-6 times too large.
  For a photon that is the substrate's own gauge mode the flux sectors are real substrate configurations (as in string-net
  models), which argues that they count. This is open and is the sharpest statement of the species-count relation so far.
* r26's w_V = 2.0 and its '0.0 % residual' are not supported; its edge-mode number is right but unused.
