A. The relation, and the exact pieces
==============================================================================
unimodular conjugacy  Delta V * Delta(Lambda/8 pi G) ~ kappa hbar   ->  Delta Lambda = 8 pi kappa l_P^2 / Delta V
Poisson substrate     Delta V = l^2 sqrt(V)       (one element per l^4)
species fix the cell  l^2 = 4 N eta l_P^2         (r14: entanglement area law = A/4G)
=> Lambda sqrt(V) = 2 pi kappa / (N eta)  ==: A_N    (dimensionless; l_P cancels)
exact past-light-cone volumes: radiation era V = 8*pi/105 t^4, matter era V = 3*pi/55 t^4
rms Omega_Lambda per unit A_N:  radiation era 4/(3 sqrt C) = 2.7253;  matter era 3/(4 sqrt C) = 1.8118
numerical LCDM check deep in radiation era: 2.7253 (exact 2.7253)
today: V_past H0^4 = 0.1229;  A_obs = Lambda_obs sqrt(V_past) = 0.7199  (Planck 2018 LCDM)

F. Inventing the box: can quieter-than-Poisson counting rescue it? (no-go for any power law)
==============================================================================
Generalise the substrate's counting statistics: Var(number in V) = (V/l^4)^gamma  (Poisson: gamma = 1;
gamma < 1 = 'hyperuniform', quieter on large scales). Then Delta V = l^4 (V/l^4)^(gamma/2) and
   Lambda = 8 pi kappa lam^(2 gamma - 4) V^(-gamma/2)   (Planck units, l = lam l_P),  Omega_Lambda ~ V^((1-gamma)/2) g(z)
today: V_past = 6.40e+242 Planck 4-volumes, Lambda_obs = 2.845e-122 (Planck units)
   cell size  1 l_P: today's Lambda needs gamma = 1.0102 (kappa 1/2) - 1.0127 (kappa 1)
   cell size  4 l_P: today's Lambda needs gamma = 1.0003 (kappa 1/2) - 1.0028 (kappa 1)
   cell size  6 l_P: today's Lambda needs gamma = 0.9974 (kappa 1/2) - 0.9999 (kappa 1)
   cell size 10 l_P: today's Lambda needs gamma = 0.9937 (kappa 1/2) - 0.9962 (kappa 1)
   cell size 30 l_P: today's Lambda needs gamma = 0.9855 (kappa 1/2) - 0.9881 (kappa 1)
   BBN (|Omega| < 0.046 at T = 1 MeV while Omega_0 = 0.685): gamma <= 0.953   [ln(V_0/V_BBN) = 160.9]
   near-constancy today: the typical (tracking) part gives w0 = -1 + gamma (dlnV/dlna)/6 with dlnV/dlna = 3.95;
   the loosest DESI bound w0 < -0.491 (Union3, 2 sigma) needs gamma <= 0.773
=> NO-GO: today's size needs gamma = 1.00 (to +-0.014 for cells of 1-30 l_P),
   BBN needs gamma <= 0.953 and today's near-constancy gamma <= 0.77. No power-law counting
   statistics gives a dark energy that is both the right size and as steady as observed. Counting-
   noise Lambda needs fluctuations that FREEZE (non-Markovian memory) -- or Lambda has another origin.

Verdict
==============================================================================
* NEW RELATION (ours): Lambda sqrt(V_past) = 2 pi kappa/(N eta). With every Standard-Model degree of
  freedom counting once, it gives today's Lambda within a factor ~1.5-3 (N needed 180-372 vs 118).
* BUT the same counting noise acts at every epoch and tracks the critical density: it makes the
  early universe fail BBN/CMB unless we are lucky at the ~1e-4 level or worse (D). This agrees with
  the difficulties reported for everpresent-Lambda models (Das et al. 2023-24) and now quantifies
  them as a function of the species number.
* DESIGN RULE for USRT dynamics: Lambda's counting noise must be suppressed in the early universe
  by a factor >~ 43 relative to white noise in V (BBN), while staying O(1) today.
* NO-GO (ours, F): quieter-than-Poisson counting with any power law cannot do it -- the size of
  Lambda pins gamma = 1.00, while BBN and DESI's near-constancy need gamma <= 0.953 and <= 0.77.
  A working version needs fluctuations with memory that freeze at late times.
