J3. One light cone for all species?
==============================================================================
    Fermion cone of this model: c_perp = Delta = 0.600, c_par = 2 t sin k_F = 2.000: anisotropy c_perp / c_par = 0.30.
    Isotropy needs Delta = 2 t sin k_F (one condition).  In the continuum chiral p-wave state c_par = v_F, c_perp = Delta_0 / p_F, so
    c_perp / c_par = Delta_0 / (2 E_F): helium-3 (Delta_0 / E_F ~ 1e-3, typed from memory) is anisotropic by a factor of ~1e3;
    isotropy needs Delta_0 = 2 mu: strong pairing, at the edge of the BCS-BEC crossover where the Weyl points approach and merge (J2).
    Other species: the sound mode (Anderson-Bogoliubov) has c_s = v_F / sqrt(3) in weak-coupling BCS (literature, not computed here),
    so c_s / c_par = 0.577 -- the phonon and the fermion cone are different cones unless the medium is tuned.
    How equal must they be?  Limits on speed differences (order of magnitude; typed from memory): gravitational waves vs light, |c_g - c_gamma| / c < ~1e-15
    (GW170817 + GRB 170817A); electron vs photon ~1e-15 or better; neutron/proton isotropy ~1e-27 (Hughes-Drever type).
    A speed mismatch of O(1) (fermion cone: 1 - 0.30 = 0.70; sound against fermion: 1 - 0.577 = 0.42) must be brought below 1e-15: the speeds must agree to 15 digits.
    Only a flow of the couplings with energy (Chadha-Nielsen 1983: emergent Lorentz invariance from renormalisation) could do it without tuning.
    If a difference delta(E) = delta(E_P) (E/E_P)^gamma, going from E_P to 1 TeV (ln = 37.0) to 1e-15 needs gamma = 0.93.
    One-loop estimates give gamma ~ g^2 / (16 pi^2) ~ 1e-3-1e-2 for couplings of standard-model size (prior art: Collins, Perez, Sudarsky, Urrutia,
    Vucetich 2004 find O(1e-3-1e-2) leftover violations for Planck-scale breaking), i.e. 1e11-1e13 above the limit.  Not computed here.
    In the flow picture of gravity (r34, r40) every species rides the same medium flow; a species with a different c deflects differently:
    rescaling H = c'|k| + v.k by c' shows it sees the flow v/c', i.e. a source of mass M c^2/c'^2: alpha' / alpha = (c / c')^2 = 1 - 2 delta.
    So the equivalence principle (and Shapiro delay) tests bound delta directly: a tuning of 1e-15 is needed even before any lumps appear.

A mass couples the two nodes (momentum transfer 2 k_F): in real space it is the amplitude to be back-scattered by a structure of the medium.
Exact test: half-filled tight-binding chain (the 1D version of the node pair, k_F = pi/2, speed 2 t), a right mover at E = 0 meets the smooth
barrier V_n = V0 / cosh^2(n/L) (L in lattice spacings; the medium's own spacing is ~ the healing length); |r| from the exact transfer matrix
