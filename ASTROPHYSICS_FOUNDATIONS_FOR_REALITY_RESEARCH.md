## 3. The Core Pillars of Relativistic Astrophysics

### 3.1 General Relativity & Einstein's Field Equations
Spacetime is a pseudo-Riemannian manifold $(M, g_{\mu\nu})$ whose curvature is governed by:
$$G_{\mu\nu} + \Lambda g_{\mu\nu} = \frac{8\pi G}{c^4} T_{\mu\nu}$$
where the Einstein tensor is $G_{\mu\nu} = R_{\mu\nu} - \frac{1}{2} R g_{\mu\nu}$.

* **The Geodesic Equation:** Free particles follow extremum paths through curved spacetime:
  $$\frac{d^2 x^\mu}{d\lambda^2} + \Gamma^\mu_{\alpha\beta} \frac{dx^\alpha}{d\lambda}\frac{dx^\beta}{d\lambda} = 0$$
* **Gravitational Lensing:** Light rays deflect by an angle:
  $$\hat{\alpha} = \frac{4 G M}{c^2 b} = \frac{2 r_s}{b}$$
  where $b$ is the impact parameter and $r_s = 2GM/c^2$ is the Schwarzschild radius. For the Sun, $\hat{\alpha} = 1.751''$, confirmed from Eddington (1919) to VLBI radio interferometry today ($10^{-5}$ accuracy).

$$ds^2 = -c^2 dt^2 + a(t)^2 \left[ \frac{dr^2}{1 - k r^2} + r^2 (d\theta^2 + \sin^2\theta d\phi^2) \right]$$
